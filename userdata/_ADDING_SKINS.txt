Adding skins

osu! skins:   userdata/skins/<skin name>/skin.ini
              (userdata/dlc/skins_osu/ works too)
Rizu skins:   userdata/dlc/skins_rizu/<skin name>/skin.lua

Unpack a skin and put its folder in one of these, so skin.ini (or skin.lua)
is directly inside the folder. Then pick it in the settings.
